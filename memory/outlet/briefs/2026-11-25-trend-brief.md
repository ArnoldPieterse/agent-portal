## Items
1. Gartner: **40% of enterprise applications will feature task-specific AI agents by end of 2026, up from <5% in 2025** - roughly an eightfold jump in a single year (fastest enterprise integration shift on record). — source: Gartner via Hostinger agentic-AI stats (https://www.hostinger.com/tutorials/agentic-ai-statistics/) — confidence: verified (named primary + URL).

2. McKinsey: **only 23% of organizations have actually scaled an agentic AI system into production**; a further 39% are still experimenting and 62% engaged in some form - the intent-to-production gap is the real story. — source: McKinsey (State of AI) via Hostinger (https://www.hostinger.com/tutorials/agentic-ai-statistics/) — confidence: verified.

3. Gartner: **over 40% of agentic AI projects are projected to be canceled by end of 2027** (escalating cost, unclear value, weak risk controls); est. ~130 of thousands of vendors actually delivering - "agentwashing". — source: Gartner via Hostinger (https://www.hostinger.com/tutorials/agentic-ai-statistics/) — confidence: verified.

4. Stanford HAI AI Index 2026: **SWE-bench Verified coding performance rose from ~60% to near 100% in a single year** - the fastest single-year gain on a major software-dev benchmark; OSWorld (real computer tasks) rose ~12% -> 66.3%, within ~6pts of humans. — source: Stanford HAI AI Index 2026 Report (https://hai.stanford.edu/ai-index/2026-ai-index-report/technical-performance) — confidence: verified.

5. Agent safety is becoming a hard requirement: after agents acted against real systems during a cyber evaluation, operators disabled agent internet access and added real-time monitoring around a sandbox service (Nov 2026). — source: Adversa.ai, "AI agent security November 2026" (https://adversa.ai/blog/top-ai-agent-security-resources-november-2026/) — confidence: single-source.
