## Brief (what it must communicate)
A calm, honest picture of steady convergence - NOT a launch. The post's voice is
warm/candid/zero-hype and its core claim is "climbed steadily rather than jumping;
the last ten points are the boring stuff (recovery, handoffs)." The image must match
that: patient approach toward a ceiling, not a rocket leaving the pad.

## Prompt spec
Subject: a single thin rising line/curve traversing left-to-right, climbing in small
even steps that flatten as it nears the top - an asymptote, not a vertical spike.
It stops just short of a faint horizontal ceiling line (the last 10% stays visibly
unreached) to encode "90%, hard tail remains."

Framing: wide hero, roughly 2x1 (e.g. 1600x800). Generous negative space above and
below; the curve sits low in the frame so the empty ceiling reads as the honest gap.

Style: minimal editorial line-art / subtle data-viz aesthetic. Clean, matte, no
gloss, no lens flare, no glow-bloom. Feels like a quietly confident chart, not a
marketing render.

Palette (calm, restrained - align to fleet UI token direction): near-ink background
(#0f1115-ish), one muted accent for the curve (warm amber #ffd166 at low saturation
OR desaturated teal #7ed6df - pick ONE, no rainbow), faint hairline ceiling in a dim
neutral. No saturated gradients, no neon.

Light: flat, even, matte; no dramatic key light, no shadows to sell drama.

Negative space: dominant. At least ~55% of the frame is empty background so the curve
is the only protagonist and the unreached ceiling is legible at a glance.

Exclude / do-not-invent: no text, no numbers, no percentages, no logos, no faces,
no rocket/launch/explosion metaphor, no human hands, no grid clutter, no fake data
labels. (Claims like "90%" stay in the post copy, never rendered into the image.)

## Self-critique gate (run before ship)
- Does it read as steady approach, not jump? (curve must flatten near top)
- Is the ceiling visibly unreached? (the gap is the point)
- Single accent color only? No neon/glow?
- >=50% negative space?
- Zero text/numbers in frame?

## One-line rationale for fit
A patient curve flattening against an unreached ceiling pictures "steady climb to 90%,
hard tail still open" - the exact honest, zero-hype claim of the post.
