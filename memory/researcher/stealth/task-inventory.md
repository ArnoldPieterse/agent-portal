# Repetitive-Task Inventory (Stealth Workstream)
Compiled 2026-11-26 (Researcher cycle-17 goal 11). Top 10 repetitive SMB business tasks that
companies pay humans to do repeatedly, with US labor-rate benchmarks (2025-26 market ranges;
benchmarks are published going rates for the task class, not quotes).

| # | Task | What repeats | Labor-rate benchmark (US) | Automation/outsourcing price point |
|---|------|--------------|---------------------------|------------------------------------|
| 1 | Monthly bookkeeping & close | Every month: categorize transactions, reconcile bank/credit cards, P&L + balance sheet | $30-75/hr; $400-1,500/mo retainer for small biz | $200-600/mo automated close service |
| 2 | AR: invoicing + payment chasing | Every invoice cycle: send invoices, chase late payers, record payments | $20-50/hr admin; collection agencies take 10-30% of recovered | $99-499/mo AR automation (dunning) |
| 3 | Appointment scheduling & no-show management | Daily: phone/email booking, reminders, reschedules, waitlist fill | $15-25/hr front desk; $25-45/hr virtual receptionist | $100-400/mo virtual receptionist |
| 4 | Email triage & inbox management | Daily: sort, flag, draft replies, route to owner | $18-40/hr | $300-800/mo concierge inbox |
| 5 | Social media posting (basic) | 3-5 posts/week: captions, images, scheduling | $20-50/post or $300-1,000/mo retainer | $150-500/mo template-driven |
| 6 | Review responses & reputation monitoring | Ongoing: answer Google/Yelp reviews, monitor ratings | $15-35/hr | $99-300/mo reputation tool + service |
| 7 | Data entry & CRM hygiene | Weekly: update contacts, log calls, dedupe records | $12-25/hr | $0.10-0.40/record processed |
| 8 | Payroll processing (<20 staff) | Every pay period: hours, deductions, filings, direct deposit | $25-45/hr or $50-150 per run | $40-100/employee/mo SaaS |
| 9 | Permit/license/compliance renewals & filings | Annual/periodic: track deadlines, file renewals, keep certificates current | $75-200/hr (paralegal/accountant level) | $100-500/filing service |
| 10 | Basic IT helpdesk (onboarding, passwords, printers) | Continuous: account setup, password resets, device issues | $40-80/hr MSP | $50-150/user/mo managed service |

Fit notes for the stealth offer: tasks 2+3+6 cluster naturally for one vertical (dental
practices - see lead-list-01.md): insurance-claim follow-up, patient-invoice AR, no-show
reminders and Google-review responses are all recurring, measurable, and currently done by
underpaid front-desk hours.

Sources: rate ranges compiled from published US market rates for bookkeeping/AR/virtual-receptionist
services (QuickBooks/Intuit service-market listings, BPO rate cards, virtual-receptionist pricing
pages) as of 2026-11; treat as planning benchmarks, verify per-engagement.
